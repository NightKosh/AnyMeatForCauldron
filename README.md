# AnyMeatForCauldron

Valheim mod adding alternative recipes with cooked meat at the cauldron,
food preparation table and mead ketill. Vanilla recipes remain available.

## Supported Cauldron Recipes

- Boar Jerky
- Minced Meat Sauce
- Sausages
- Turnip Stew
- Wolf Jerky
- Wolf Meat Skewer
- Seeker Aspic
- Fiery Svinstew
- Mashed Meat
- Oven Pancake Batter
- Meat In Bread
- Seal Meat Soup
- Smoked Moose Meat
- Meatballs and Poteitr
- Smoked Fish
- Fish Soup

## Supported Food Preparation Table Recipes

- Unbaked Lox Pie
- Uncooked Meat Platter
- Uncooked Misthare Supreme
- Uncooked Honey Glazed Chicken- 
- Uncooked Piquant Pie

## Supported Mead Ketill Recipes

- Mead: Poison Resist

## Configs
- Each dish recipe can be disabled.
- `ModEnabled` disables all alternative recipes.

## Requirements

- Valheim 1.0 or later
- BepInEx
- Jötunn
